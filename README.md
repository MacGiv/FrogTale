# FrogTale

A poisonous tide is swallowing your village. In Frogtale, you play as a heroic frog on a desperate mission to save your people. Use your tongue and bow to grapple, pull, and break every obstacle in your path as you fight your way to the purifying spring!

Inspired by classic Game Boy games, this 2D top-down action-adventure offers a challenging new journey with retro charm.

This is a game being developed by students from <a href="https://www.imagecampus.edu.ar/">Image Campus</a>
